WL-aware policy comparison - controlled runtime validation
Date: 2026-08-27

Geometry
- 1 channel, 1 LUN, 1 plane
- 64 blocks, 256 pages/block
- 4 KiB/page
- TLC abstraction: 3 FEMU pages per modeled WL (86 WLs/block)

Workload
- Fill one 256-page line.
- Repeatedly read page 30 (modeled WL 10) for 256 direct reads.
- The adjacent victim WLs are WL 9 and WL 11.

BLOCK policy
- rd_reclaim_policy=0
- rd_reclaim_threshold=256
Observed:
  RD retry: blk=0 pg=30 reads=177 ... retries=1 total=1
  RD reclaim: line=0 trigger_blk=0 reads=256 pages=256 erases=1 events=1
Result: 256 valid pages migrated and the block was erased.

STRAW-inspired WL-aware policy
- rd_reclaim_policy=1
- rd_pages_per_wl=3
- rd_straw_alpha_x1000=8400 (alpha=8.4)
- rd_straw_erc_max=2150
- rd_straw_check_interval=8
Observed:
  RD STRAW WL reclaim: blk=0 wl=9 erc=2150.4 pages=3 total_pages=3
  RD STRAW WL reclaim: blk=0 wl=11 erc=2150.4 pages=3 total_pages=6
  RD STRAW event: blk=0 reads=256 wls=2 pages=6 events=1
Result: only the two adjacent victim WLs were reclaimed, for 6 migrated pages total. No immediate block erase was performed by the selective-reclaim path.

Comparison
- BLOCK: 256 migrated pages, 1 erase
- STRAW-inspired: 6 migrated pages, 0 immediate erase
- Page-copy reduction in this controlled validation workload: 250/256 = 97.65625%

Scope
This is a mechanism-level comparison under a scaled, controlled FEMU validation configuration. It is not a reproduction of STRAW's published performance numbers or a calibrated NAND-device evaluation.
